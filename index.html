LunarLeaf-CFD is a browser-based fluid-dynamics workbench for modelling and visualising the airflow and O2 / CO2 / H2O concentration gradients that form around plant tissue — and how they change as gravity changes (1 g → Mars 0.38 g → Moon 0.17 g → microgravity). Part of the AstroBotany / AIRI family and the Lunar Leaf line of work. Code & docs on GitHub.
