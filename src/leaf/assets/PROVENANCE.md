# Leaf asset provenance

Every file in this directory is geometry that something downstream will treat as a leaf.
This page records where each one came from, what it is safe to claim about it, and the
open questions that gate replacing them with real ones.

## Current contents — all provisional

| asset | blade | source |
|---|---|---|
| `provisional-mature-flat` | 15.0 × 7.9 mm, 72.5 mm² | analytic |
| `provisional-mature-cupped` | 15.0 × 7.9 mm, 72.5 mm² | analytic (same outline, posed) |
| `provisional-juvenile` | 6.0 × 4.0 mm, 15.0 mm² | analytic |
| `provisional-cotyledon` | 2.5 × 2.0 mm, 3.3 mm² | analytic |

**None of these is a fit to real imagery.** They are beta-function half-width sweeps,
`w(y) ∝ (y/L)^a (1 − y/L)^b`, generated by `tools/leaf-assets/make_provisional.mjs`,
sized to ordinary Col-0 dimensions. `provisional-mature-flat` is set to 15 mm so it
matches this repo's existing calibration (T3: leaf 1.5 cm ↔ 52 lattice cells) and can be
compared like-for-like against the `stampEllipse` reference geometry.

They carry `"provisional": true`, which surfaces as a warning in the AeroLeaf UI, a line
in every exported OBJ header, a caption on the contour images, and a `(provisional)` tag
in the LunarLeaf scenario readouts. **No published number should rest on them.**

## Replacing them with real leaves

`tools/neuraleaf/` has the pipeline:

1. `segment_plate.py` — plate photo → per-component masks + QC overlay. Verified against
   `Gravitropism_flashlapse_/Gravi_02_0090.jpg`: it isolates the three seedlings' shoots
   and correctly ignores the roots, the QR card and the dish.
2. `export_assets.py` — one mask → an asset JSON with a real, correctly scaled outline,
   midrib and EXIF-derived timestamp. Verified end-to-end.
3. `build_series.py` — tracks one component across a timelapse and writes an asset per
   frame plus `age_series.csv`. Tested on Gravi_02; see open question 1b for what it found.
4. `fit_leaves.py` — *optional*. Adds the out-of-plane pose that a top-down silhouette
   cannot contain, using NeuraLeaf. See the caveats below.

Validate anything new with `node validation/leaf_inspect.mjs <asset.json>` before adding
it to `registry.ts`, then re-run `tools/sync-leaf-module.sh` to propagate to AeroLeaf.

## Open question 1 — the age axis is shorter than it looks

`Gravitropism_flashlapse_/` is the obvious local source of age-stamped Arabidopsis. Its
EXIF timestamps say what it actually is:

- 96 frames, `Gravi_02_0000` … `Gravi_02_0095`
- 15.03 min cadence
- **23.79 h total span** (2017-06-12 17:44:26 → 2017-06-13 17:32:04)

That is a **one-day gravitropism series**, not a development series. It supports an axis
labelled *"hours since imaging start"* and nothing more — it cannot support claims about
leaf ageing, expansion stages or senescence, and asset notes must not imply otherwise.

For a real age axis, `TIMELAPSE_PIPELINE_STATUS.md` lists better candidates in the CyVerse
store: `Day1-5_BW_jpg` (216 frames over 5 days), `ABCD_1` (485 frames), and
`FlashLapse_images_for_CyVerse/Straight_growth` (612 frames).

Note also that these are **seedlings** — cotyledons and first true leaves, a few mm across.
That is the right stage for the BRIC/CARA Petri-dish scenarios this repo models, but it is
not a mature rosette leaf.

## Open question 1b — thresholding cannot separate these leaves (tested)

`build_series.py` was run across `Gravi_02`, tracking the middle plant over 12 frames /
22 h. It tracked cleanly (0 frames lost) but **every tracked component was a whole-plant
cluster, never a single leaf** — solidity 0.34–0.48, because the cotyledons and first true
leaves overlap. Excess-green thresholding separates plants from background; it does not
separate leaves from each other.

So per-leaf outlines from this footage need one of:

- **manual annotation** of a handful of frames, or
- **NeuraLeaf's base-shape prior** via `fit_leaves.py` — which is exactly the job that
  prior exists to do, and is blocked on the licence question below.

The tool refuses to write a leaf asset for a cluster; `--force-cluster-asset` will write
one, marked `provisional` and labelled a whole-plant silhouette. Do not pass that flag and
then call the result a leaf.

What the run *did* produce is real plant-level morphometrics (in `age_series.csv`):
projected shoot area 9.08 → 13.73 mm² over 18 h (+51%), then falling to 10.79 mm² by 22 h,
with bounding length and width crossing over mid-series. The crossover and the late decline
look like reorientation and leaf folding rather than growth — unsurprising in a
gravitropism experiment where the plate may have been re-oriented.

## Open question 1c — the image scale is assumed, not measured

Every millimetre above rests on **24.4 px/mm**, derived from the dish spanning ~2440 px on
the assumption that it is a **100 mm** dish. That was not confirmed. The dish is Falcon-
branded and Falcon rounds come in 35/60/100/150 mm; the alternatives give 24.4 px/mm (100 mm)
or 27.1 px/mm (90 mm) or 40.7 px/mm (60 mm), and every reported area scales as the square of
whichever is right. **Confirm the dish diameter before any of these numbers are used.**
Attempts to derive the scale from the slide/QR card in frame were inconclusive — it is
tilted, and its printed red border is not a known dimension.

## Open question 2 — NeuraLeaf licensing

The pose step uses NeuraLeaf:

> Yang, Mao, Santo, Matsushita & Okura (2025). *NeuraLeaf: Neural Parametric Leaf Models
> with Shape and Deformation Disentanglement.* ICCV 2025. arXiv:2507.12714
> <https://github.com/Yrainy0615/NeuraLeaf>

**That repository carries no LICENSE file**, so its code and released checkpoints are
under default copyright — all rights reserved. (The project *website* is CC BY-SA 4.0,
which covers the website, and would in any case be incompatible with this repo's MIT
licence if applied to derived assets.)

Consequences, which are already respected in this repo:

- No NeuraLeaf code or checkpoint is vendored here. `fit_leaves.py` shells out to a
  checkout **you** obtain, at a path **you** provide via `$NEURALEAF_HOME`.
- The outline pipeline (`segment_plate.py` → `export_assets.py`) does **not** use
  NeuraLeaf at all, so outlines from your own imagery are unencumbered.
- Only the `profile` / `camber` tables and the `neuraleaf` block that `fit_leaves.py`
  writes are derived from it.

**Before depositing on Zenodo or publishing figures made from NeuraLeaf-derived geometry,
settle redistribution with the authors.** Until then, prefer assets whose pose is either
flat or explicitly imposed by hand (`export_assets.py --curl/--cup`, which records that
the pose was imposed rather than measured).

## Coordinate convention

Millimetres throughout. `x` transverse across the blade, `y` along the midrib with the
petiole junction at 0, `z` out of the flattened plane. `outline` and `midrib` are the
flattened 2D base shape; `profile` (midrib height vs normalised arclength) and `camber`
(cupping vs normalised transverse position at the widest chord) carry the deformation.
The split follows NeuraLeaf's, so one outline can be re-posed without re-running anything.
